## Overview

Version 0.3.0 achieves 100% complete coverage across all **31,010 textures** extracted directly from the internal archives (`.mld` and `.mlk`) of the GameCube disc image.

All textures have been fully converted into **Microsoft DirectX BC7 DDS** format with full hardware mipmaps (`-m 0` / `-m 1`). Unlike standard PNG texture packs that cause micro-stuttering while your CPU decodes images at runtime, BC7 DDS textures bypass CPU decoding and load directly into GPU VRAM using Dolphin's custom texture prefetching pipeline.

### Highlights in v0.3.0
* **100% GameCube ISO Coverage**: Every character, ship, town, dungeon, overworld continent, battle arena, and cutscene asset from the raw disc image is fully upscaled and curated.
* **Alpha Channel & Ghost Haze Audit**: Systematic removal of low-alpha edge fringing ($1 \le \alpha \le 25$) to eliminate gray bounding-box outlines on character cutouts and foliage.
* **Standardized Atmospheric Pipeline**: Transparent overlays (clouds, smoke, mist, fog, and heat haze) upscaled with proportional Gaussian blur strictly on the alpha channel to preserve soft gradients without pixel stepping.
* **Runtime GPU Textures**: Dynamic Format 8 (C4, 4-bit paletted) textures generated during endgame bosses, ship battles, and Intro Movie A extracted and remastered with dual `_arb.dds` compatibility aliases.

## How to Install on Dolphin Emulator

### 1. Download & Extract
* Download the desired `.7z` packages from the table above or the [Releases page](https://github.com/shoopkid/Skies-of-Arcadia-Legends-HD-Textures-Pack/releases).
* Select the downloaded `.7z` files, right-click, and choose **7-Zip → Extract Here**.
* All archives unpack into a unified **`GEA`** directory (the GameCube title ID for *Skies of Arcadia Legends*).

### 2. Move to Dolphin's Textures Directory
Copy or move the extracted **`GEA`** folder into Dolphin's texture load path:
* **Windows:** `Documents\Dolphin Emulator\Load\Textures\`
* **macOS:** `~/Library/Application Support/Dolphin/Load/Textures/`
* **Linux:** `~/.local/share/dolphin-emu/Load/Textures/`

The folder structure inside `Load/Textures/` will look like this:
```text
Load/Textures/GEA/
  ├── 01 - Characters & Enemies/
  ├── 02 - Towns & Ports/
  ├── 03 - Dungeons & Ruins/
  ├── 04 - Ships & Ship Battles/
  ├── 05 - Overworld/
  ├── 06 - Battle Arenas & Effects/
  ├── 07 - UI, Menus & Fonts/
  └── 08 - Cutscenes & Ending/
```

### 3. Configure Dolphin Settings
1. Open Dolphin Emulator and click **Graphics**.
2. On the **Advanced** tab:
   * Check **Load Custom Textures**.
   * Check **Prefetch Custom Textures** *(Preloads BC7 DDS directly into GPU VRAM on startup to eliminate runtime hitching).*
3. On the **Enhancements** tab:
   * Internal Resolution: **3x (1080p)**, **4x (1440p)**, or **5x (4K)**.
   * Anisotropic Filtering: **8x** or **16x**.
   * Check **Arbitrary Mipmap Detection**.
   * Check **Force 24-Bit Color**.
   * Check **Disable Copy Filter**.
   * Uncheck **Disable Fog** *(ensures in-game depth mist and fog render correctly).*
4. On the **General** tab:
   * Backend: **Vulkan** or **Direct3D 12**.
