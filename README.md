## Overview

Version 0.3.1 builds upon the 100% complete disc image coverage across all **31,439 textures** with curated visual restorations, face remasters, and game-wide transparency fixes.

All textures are provided in **Microsoft DirectX BC7 DDS** format with hardware mipmaps (`-m 0` / `-m 1`). BC7 DDS textures bypass CPU decoding overhead and stream directly into GPU VRAM using Dolphin's custom texture prefetching pipeline.

### Highlights in v0.3.1
* **Albatross Crew & Citizen Face Remasters**: Remastered facial expressions and features for Dyne, Briggs, Timmus, Patch, Boris, Alan, Lindsi, Peter, Luke, Mabel, and Martha using Japanese Dreamcast master textures.
* **Party Face Restorations**: Upgraded lower-resolution facial expression textures for Vyse, Aika, and Fina, restored Vyse's belt buckle geometry, and color-matched scarf highlights.
* **Galcian Uniform & Alfonso Smile**: Stripped improper transparency causing see-through meshes on Galcian's uniform, restored clipped gold trim, and cleaned up Alfonso's smiling face texture.
* **Combat Portraits Overhaul**: Cleaned background white-bleed noise on battle portrait badges to eliminate compression edge halos, and enforced single-level mipmaps for razor-sharp 2D battle UI.
* **Cutscene & Environmental Restorations**: Removed halo artifacts around the Intro Movie Green Moon, repaired Ixa'taka cloud clipping, reconstructed the Valuan flagship crest and flag, repaired Albatross circular viewports, and added volumetric steam hazes in Temple of Pyrynn Area 2.
* **Game-Wide Transparency Purge**: Cleaned low-alpha fringes and background color bleed across over 500 textures (magic spells, boss decals, foliage, lanterns, and ironwork) to eliminate square outlines and glowing borders.

See the full [v0.3.1 Release Notes](release_notes_v0.3.1.md) for details.

## How to Install on Dolphin Emulator

### 1. Download & Extract
* Download the desired `.7z` packages from the table above or the [Releases page](https://github.com/shoopkid/Skies-of-Arcadia-Legends-HD-Textures-Pack/releases).
* Select the downloaded `.7z` files, right-click, and choose **7-Zip → Extract Here**.
* All archives unpack into a unified **`GEA`** directory (the GameCube title ID for *Skies of Arcadia Legends*).

### 2. Move to Dolphin's Textures Directory
Copy or move the extracted **`GEA`** folder into Dolphin's texture load path:
* **Windows:** `Documents\Dolphin Emulator\Load\Textures\`
* **macOS:** `~/Library/Application Support/Dolphin/Load/Textures/`
* **Linux:** `~/.local/share/dolphin-emu/Load/Textures/`

The folder structure inside `Load/Textures/` will look like this:
```text
Load/Textures/GEA/
  ├── 01 - Characters & Enemies/
  ├── 02 - Towns & Ports/
  ├── 03 - Dungeons & Ruins/
  ├── 04 - Ships & Ship Battles/
  ├── 05 - Overworld/
  ├── 06 - Battle Arenas & Effects/
  ├── 07 - UI, Menus & Fonts/
  └── 08 - Cutscenes & Ending/
```

### 3. Configure Dolphin Settings
1. Open Dolphin Emulator and click **Graphics**.
2. On the **Advanced** tab:
   * Check **Load Custom Textures**.
   * Check **Prefetch Custom Textures** *(Preloads BC7 DDS directly into GPU VRAM on startup to eliminate runtime hitching).*
3. On the **Enhancements** tab:
   * Internal Resolution: **3x (1080p)**, **4x (1440p)**, or **5x (4K)**.
   * Anisotropic Filtering: **8x** or **16x**.
   * Check **Arbitrary Mipmap Detection**.
   * Check **Force 24-Bit Color**.
   * Check **Disable Copy Filter**.
   * Uncheck **Disable Fog** *(ensures in-game depth mist and fog render correctly).*
4. On the **General** tab:
   * Backend: **Vulkan** or **Direct3D 12**.
